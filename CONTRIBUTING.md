# Contributing

## Contributor License Agreement (CLA)

When you propose a pull request on this repository you must execute a Khronos
[Open Source Contributor License Agreement
(CLA)](https://www.khronos.org/cla/KhronosGroup/VK-GL-CTS), to confirm
you own your work and are granting Khronos the necessary permissions to
redistribute it under our licenses.

## AI-Assisted Contributions

By submitting a Contribution to this repository, you additionally represent
that, to the extent any of Your Contributions were developed with the assistance
of artificial intelligence tools or AI-generated code, You have exercised
sufficient review, judgment, and creative direction over such tools and
resulting material to reasonably consider it Your original creation, and You are
not aware of any third-party license, intellectual property claim, or other
restriction arising from such use that is associated with any part of Your
Contribution or use thereof.

## Practical Advice and Technical Details

To contribute to VK-GL-CTS, Khronos members should use the [Khronos Gitlab
instance](https://gitlab.khronos.org/Tracker/vk-gl-cts/) and its [corresponding
wiki](https://gitlab.khronos.org/Tracker/vk-gl-cts/wikis/home), which explains
how to submit changes through gerrit. Non-Khronos members should use [the
project's GitHub site](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/VK-GL-CTS) and its
[corresponding wiki](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/VK-GL-CTS/wiki), and use
pull requests for contributions.

For OpenGL and OpenGL ES, `external/openglcts/CONTRIBUTING.md` contains
technical and architectural details related to those tests, which should help
developers who are not familiar with the code.

For Vulkan, `external/vulkancts/CONTRIBUTING.md` contains the same kind of
information.
